    FAQ

    Questions shippers ask us

    Cannot find your answer? Call the head office on +91 9008898260.

    Are you a broker or do you own your trucks?

    Both, and that is the point. We are fleet owners first, which gives us direct control over pricing and timelines. As transport contractors and commission agents we also bring in trusted partner vehicles when a job needs more capacity.

    Can you move over dimensional cargo?

    Yes. ODC and project cargo is a core specialty. We have moved reactors, boilers, transformers, wagons and heavy machinery on low beds and multi axle trailers, including to remote sites where we build temporary roads for access.

    Which vehicle sizes do you run?

    Pickups, 19 FT canters, 20 FT to 32 FT SXL and MXL containers, 32 FT JCB platform trucks, 40 FT trailers, 50 FT and 60 FT trailers, and low beds for heavy ODC.

    How do I track my shipment?

    Every owned vehicle carries GPS with geo fencing, alerts and real time ETA. Hired vehicles are tracked through phone tracking. We can also push tracking into your own system through APIs.

    Which regions do you serve?

    We run pan India from our Bangalore head office and nine branches. Regular coverage includes Gujarat, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Jharkhand, Bihar, West Bengal, Assam, Mumbai and the East Zone.
